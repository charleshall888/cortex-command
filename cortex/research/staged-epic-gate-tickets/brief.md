The research settled on treating this as a cluster of concrete backlog-hygiene and epic-closure fixes rather than as an adoption of staged decomposition. Five pieces are chartered: read-time recognition of finished statuses so completed items stop rendering as active, visibility for work parked via the status field, collapsing the five competing status vocabularies into one authoritative set, epic-closure truthfulness so a parent reflects its children's real terminal values and late children become audible, and prevention of work against a superseded understanding — with that last piece charted but its mechanism deliberately left unchosen.

Several other directions were weighed and set aside. New ticket types for decision or requirements work were rejected because routing keys on readiness, not type, and four independent workflow systems model "requires a human" as a state or attribute. A dedicated investigation fast path failed all three of its tests — spikes are already cheaper, the escape hatch already exists, and the two exemplars were correct outcomes — so it is held pending at least five genuinely bounded read-only investigations with a nameable downstream harm. A body-write verb was rejected because bodies are already editable and re-authored routinely. Simply prompting agents to reconcile downstream tickets was rejected as prose-only enforcement of exactly the sequential gate whose deviation is the reported failure.

The accepted cost is that the fifth piece ships no mechanism yet. Interrupting via a blocking reconciliation gate guarantees a stop but not a rewrite; withholding authorship makes decay impossible but requires discovery to emit an incomplete piece set against a governing accepted decision. Choosing between them waits on a corpus census that only the epic-visibility fix makes possible — so the reported decay stays live until then.
