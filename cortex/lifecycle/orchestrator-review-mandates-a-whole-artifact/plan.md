## Risks

- **The serial chain is unavoidable, not a restructure signal.** All three tasks edit one 2756-byte prose file, so every edge is a write-serialization edge and the level count equals the task count. There is no seam to introduce — a registration hub makes no sense for prose — so the annotated edges are the honest remedy rather than something to flatten. Trunk dispatch is therefore no worse than a worktree here.
- **The byte trim may be infeasible.** ~180 B is a 12% cut of the pre-§3 region. If it cannot be found without losing control flow or touching §1's definition, the spec's edge case directs a halt rather than a pin raise — the pin was already raised once on 2026-08-03 for #433, and a second raise the same week is the erosion the ratchet exists to prevent.
- **Task 2 carries two silent traps** (column-zero `verdict: failed`, and a second envelope block) that pass their own requirement while breaking another. Both are called out in its Context; a builder skimming only the Verification list could still trip them.
- **The proportionality premise remains unmeasured.** Phase 1 rests on verified rationale-rot findings; Phase 2 rests on a single incident in which nothing was actually lost. Dropping Phase 2 and shipping Phase 1 alone stays coherent if that trade reads badly at implement time.
